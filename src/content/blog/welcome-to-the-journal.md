Every book leaves a few thoughts on the cutting-room floor. This journal is
where they get to live.

Consider it a room off to the side of the story — somewhere to share news about
the book, unpick the ideas underneath it, and pass along the small, lovely
things I stumble across while writing. Some entries will be about *Not Now
Dear* directly. Others will wander.

## What to expect

A little of everything, in the spirit of the book itself:

- **News** — where to find the book, events, and the occasional milestone.
- **Behind the pages** — the ideas, characters and coincidences that shaped it.
- **Gathered thoughts** — essays on attention, boundaries, and making room for
  the life you actually want.

> There is always time for a good story.

If any of that sounds like your kind of thing, the best way to keep up is to
join the readers below. You'll get a quiet note now and then — never noise.

Thanks for being here at the beginning.
