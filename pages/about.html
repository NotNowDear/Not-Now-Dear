A story with a little bite.

Not Now Dear

What happens when “later” becomes a life?

Not Now Dear follows the tender and complicated business of making a life that feels like your own. It is for anyone who has ever been asked to be smaller, quieter, or more convenient.

At its heart, it is a story about friendship, family, and the quiet bravery of deciding what deserves your attention. Funny, intimate, and full of warmth, it invites readers to pause—and then to choose themselves.

“For anyone learning to make room for their own voice.”
